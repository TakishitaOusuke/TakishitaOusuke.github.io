About

I'm Ousuke Takishita, a manufacturing engineer and maker in Japan. For work I build welding-robot automation; in my own time I build with 3D printers, Arduino / ESP32 electronics and small web tools, and I have published build logs and videos in Japanese for years.

This site is the English companion to that maker work: research-based comparisons of the website builders and creator tools people use to publish their projects. Comparisons are based on official vendor documentation unless an article explicitly says a product was tested hands-on.
